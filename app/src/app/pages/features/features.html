Offline

Nothing lost when the Wi-Fi drops

If a board loses its connection it holds on to its readings and sends them when it is back. Commands you send meanwhile wait for it.

  • Readings held on the board and sent later
  • Late readings keep the time they were taken
  • Commands wait for the device to come back
