Privacy policy

Your data, kept simple.

WBSKT keeps what it needs to look after your projects, and nothing more. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you around the web. This policy says what we collect, why, who else handles it, and what you can ask us to do with it.

Last updated 11 November 2026 · Read it with our Terms of service

Who we are

WBSKT ("we", "us") runs this website, the WBSKT console and the services your boards connect to. We decide how your personal data is used for those services, which makes us its controller under laws such as the GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act.

For anything about your data, email privacy@wbskt.com.

What we collect

Your account. Your name, your email address and a hashed copy of your password. We never see or store the password itself.

Your workspaces. Your clients' names, the readings and messages they send, when they come and go online, and the rules, workflows, policies and integrations you create. Secrets you give an integration, such as a bot token, are stored encrypted.

Your team. The people you invite, their email addresses and their roles.

Billing. Your plan, what you've paid and your invoices. Card and bank details go straight to our payment provider and never reach us.

Technical and security records. IP addresses, sign-in attempts, the browser or board that connected, and an audit log of what changed in a workspace and who changed it.

What you tell us. Emails and support requests you send us.

Why we use it

To run the service you signed up for. Connecting your boards, running your workflows, sending the messages you set up and showing you your data. This is the contract between us.

To keep it safe. Spotting abuse, refusing bad sign-ins and investigating problems. This is our legitimate interest, and yours.

To bill you and keep records. Tax and accounting law requires us to keep payment records.

To tell you what you need to know. Sign-in and invite emails, warnings when you near a plan limit, and notice of changes to these terms. We don't send marketing email unless you ask for it.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you that have legal effects.

Each plan keeps readings and run history for a set time, listed on the plans page. Older data is deleted automatically. When you delete your account, it is removed from the live service straight away and drops out of backups within 14 days.

Who else handles it

Our hosting provider. WBSKT runs on Microsoft Azure, which stores the data on our behalf and can't use it for anything else.

Our email provider. It delivers sign-in, invite, password reset and warning emails.

Dodo Payments. Our merchant of record takes payments, charges sales tax or VAT and issues invoices. It handles your payment details under its own privacy policy.

The places you send messages. When a workflow messages Telegram, Discord, ntfy, an email address or a URL, that service receives the message. You choose which ones to connect, and their own policies apply.

Google Fonts. This website loads its fonts from Google, which sees your IP address when it sends them.

When the law requires it. We disclose data only when a valid legal order makes us, and we'll tell you unless we're forbidden to. If WBSKT is ever sold or merged, your data moves only under this policy, and we'll tell you first.

Nobody else. There are no advertising or analytics companies involved.

Where it's stored

Our servers may be in a different country from you. Where the law asks for it, we transfer data under safeguards such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How we protect it

Everything travels over TLS. Passwords are hashed and integration secrets are encrypted. Only the people who run WBSKT can reach the servers, and every change in a workspace is logged. No system is perfectly secure, so if a breach puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the regulators the law requires us to tell.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, or give you a copy to take elsewhere. You can also object to how we use it, ask us to limit it, or withdraw consent you gave. Email privacy@wbskt.com from the address you signed up with and we'll answer within 30 days.

You can remove clients, workflows, integrations and team members in the console whenever you like. To delete your account and everything in it, use your account settings in the console or email us.

If you're unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies.

The console sets one cookie to keep you signed in and keeps your sign-in and unsaved workflow drafts in your browser's storage. All of it is needed for the console to work, and none of it tracks you.

Children

WBSKT isn't meant for anyone under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you think a child has signed up, email us and we'll delete the account.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll email you at least 30 days before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Questions are welcome at privacy@wbskt.com or on GitHub.
