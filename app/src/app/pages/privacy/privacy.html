Privacy

Your data, kept simple.

WBSKT keeps what it needs to look after your projects, and nothing more. We don't sell it, we don't show ads, and we don't track you around the web.

Last updated 31 October 2026

What we keep

Your account. Your name, your email address and a scrambled (hashed) copy of your password. We never see the password itself.

Your clients. Their names, the readings and messages they send, and when they come and go online.

Your setup. The rules, workflows, policies and integrations you create, and who you've invited.

An audit log. A record of what changed in your workspace and who changed it, so you can look back.

How long we keep it

Readings30 days
Activity history90 days
Warnings and errors1 year
Raw device traffic2 days
Your accountUntil you delete it

Older data is deleted automatically. You can download a client's readings as a CSV file from the console at any time.

Who else sees it

The places you send messages. When a workflow messages you on Telegram, Discord or email, that service receives the message. You choose which ones to connect.

Our email provider. It delivers sign-in, invite and password reset emails to your address.

Nobody else. We don't sell or share your data, and there are no advertising or analytics companies involved.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies. It loads its fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when it sends them.

The console uses one cookie to keep you signed in. That's it.

Your choices

You can remove clients, workflows, integrations and team members whenever you like, and they're gone.

To delete your account and everything in it, email privacy@wbskt.com from the address you signed up with. We'll confirm when it's done.

Questions

WBSKT is built in the open. Ask anything on GitHub, or email privacy@wbskt.com. If this page changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you by email first.
