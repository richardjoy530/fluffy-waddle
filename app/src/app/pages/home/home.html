How it works

Three small steps. Most people are done in about ten minutes.

01

Connect your board

Add a few lines to your sketch. Your board joins Wi‑Fi and starts sending readings.

02

Set a rule

Choose what to watch and what should happen, like "when it drops below 2 °C".

03

Get on with your day

WBSKT sends you a message, switches something on, or both. You only hear from it when it matters.

A few lines of code

Add your key and your Wi‑Fi, then send whatever your sensor measures.

Arduino & ESP32Raspberry Pi.NET
greenhouse.ino Copy
#include <Wbskt.h>

Wbskt wbskt("your-device-key");

void setup() {
  wbskt.begin("home-wifi", "wifi-password");
}

void loop() {
  float temp = readTemperature();
  wbskt.send("reading", "temp", temp);
  delay(60000);
}
