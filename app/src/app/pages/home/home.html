Durable

It doesn't lose its place.

Each step is saved as it runs. A failed step tries again, a restart picks up where it stopped, and every run is kept.

Wi‑Fi drops

Your board reconnects and carries on. The rule runs on WBSKT, so it was never waiting on your board.

Failed steps try again

A step that fails is retried, and a restart resumes the run at the step it stopped on.

30 days of readings

Every reading is kept for a month, so you can look back at last week's cold snap.

How it works

Three small steps. Most people are done in about ten minutes.

01

Connect your board

Add a few lines to your sketch. Your board joins Wi‑Fi and starts sending readings.

02

Set a rule

Choose what to watch and what should happen, like "when it drops below 2 °C".

03

Get on with your day

WBSKT sends you a message, switches something on, or both. You only hear from it when it matters.

A few lines of code

Add your key and your Wi‑Fi, then send whatever your sensor measures.

Ideas to start with

Small projects that are nice to have running in the background.

Frost alert

When the greenhouse stays below 2 °C for 10 minutes, turn the heater on, check again in 15 minutes and message me.

Garage left open

When the door is still open after 10 pm, close it. If it doesn't report closed within a minute, message me.

Thirsty plants

When the soil has been dry for an hour, run the pump for 20 seconds. Still dry 10 minutes later? Message me.

Print finished

When the 3D printer goes quiet, send an email.
