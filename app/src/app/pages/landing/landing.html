Getting started

How it works

01 · Connect

Open a connection and keep it open

Our C#, Python and JavaScript SDKs hold a persistent connection from any device, gateway or backend. Events arrive the moment they happen — no polling loops, no cold starts.

02 · Automate

Build the logic in the workflow editor

Triggers, conditions, delays and joins. Runs keep their own state, so a workflow can wait minutes or days without losing its place or burning compute.

03 · Act

Send commands back to your hardware

Control any connected device over the same connection it reports on, or reach outside services through sandboxed marketplace integrations.
