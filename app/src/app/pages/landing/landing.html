How it works

01 · Connect

Open a socket, keep it open

C#, Python & JS SDKs hold a persistent WebSocket from any device or service. No polling, no cold starts.

02 · Orchestrate

Wire logic in the workflow IDE

Triggers, conditions, delays, joins. Runs are stateful — they pause, resume and survive restarts.

03 · Act

Command anything downstream

Fire commands back down the same socket, or through sandboxed marketplace integrations.

Built for the always-on.

Ten lines to a live line.

Register a client, subscribe to a channel, act on commands. The SDK handles reconnects, backoff and resumption.
