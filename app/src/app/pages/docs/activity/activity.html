Audit log

The audit log records what happened in a workspace: who signed in, which client joined, who changed a policy, which workflow was published and which run sent a command. Use it to answer "who did that, and when?".

What it shows

Open Audit Logs from the activity rail on the left. The explorer lists views, each a slice of the same log:

ViewHolds
All activityEverything below.
People and accessSign-ins, failed or not, password changes and resets, invitations, members removed, suspended or restored, roles given or changed.
ClientsJoins, approvals, renames, status changes and commands.
PoliciesPolicies created, changed, turned off and on, and full.
WorkflowsPublishes, runs started by hand and cancelled, deprecations, deletions and shared variables set.
IntegrationsIntegrations added, tested and replaced.
SecurityUnder Needs a look: the warnings about failed sign-ins, keys and PINs rotated, deletions and boards turned away.
Each personWhat one member did.
Wbskt (system)What WBSKT did on its own, like ending a stuck run or noticing a limit.

Each entry has its time, what happened, who did it, where it came from, and how serious it is. Open one to see everything it carries, including what changed: a policy's limit from 10 to 20, a client's name, a workflow's version.

The log is Live: new entries appear as they happen. Pause it to read without the list moving.

Client and policy pages show their own activity too, which is this log narrowed to them.

Filters

  • Search finds text anywhere in an entry: a name, an ID, a value.
  • Who narrows it to one person. Resource narrows it to one client, policy or workflow.
  • Severity: All, Warnings and up, or Errors only.
  • Source: where it came from.
  • The range: the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or 90 days.
SourceMeans
ConsoleSomeone did it in the console.
APISomeone did it with their token from somewhere else: a script, another tool.
ClientA board did it: connected, sent a message, answered a command.
WorkflowA run did it: sent a command, or set a shared variable. The entry names the run.
SystemWBSKT did it on its own.
SupportWBSKT support changed something for you, with the reason they gave.

Client traffic

Messages, replies and pings are client traffic. There is a lot of it, so it is kept for 2 days, and you can hide it with the Client traffic switch to see only the changes. Everything else is kept for 90 days.

Export

Export CSV downloads what the log lists, with the same filters, newest first. A file holds up to 100,000 rows; one cut there says so on its last line, so choose a shorter range.

What happens when…

A board keeps trying to join a full policy
The log says the policy is full at most once an hour, so a retrying board doesn't flood it. Every try is still refused.
A workflow sends a command to a client
The entry's source is Workflow, it names the run, and it shows under that workflow as well as the client.
Someone signs in with the wrong password
A failed sign-in is logged in every workspace the person belongs to, with the address and browser it came from, and shows under Security.
A member is removed from the workspace
What they did stays in the log, under their name.
I need something from four months ago
It's gone: entries are kept 90 days. Export the log regularly if you need a longer record.
In depth

Rules and API

Endpoints

Under /api/v1/workspaces/{ws}/event-logs, all with logs.read.

CallDoes
GET /Entries newest first, limit per page (50 by default). Filters, all combined: group, eventNames, eventName (part of a name), criticality, minCriticality, source, policyRefId, clientRefId, workflowRefId (every version, deleted ones too), userRefId, from/to, traffic=include|exclude, q (up to 100 characters), and sinceId for only what is newer.
GET summaryCounts per group, event, person and source over from/to (the last 7 days by default, at most 400).
GET csvThe same filters as one file: id,createdAt,event,criticality,userRefId,clientRefId,policyRefId,workflowRefId,data,source,clientAddress,userAgent. The last 7 days by default, at most 400, at most 100,000 rows.

Groups are people, clients, policies, workflows, integrations and security. Sources are Console, Api, Client, Workflow, System and Support.

Errors

CodeStatusWhen
EVENT_LOG_GROUP_UNKNOWN400A group that isn't one of the above.
EVENT_LOG_SOURCE_UNKNOWN400A source that isn't one of the above.
CLIENT_NOT_FOUND, POLICY_NOT_FOUND404A client or policy filter this workspace doesn't have.
RATE_LIMITED429More than 10 exports a minute in the workspace, shared with the readings export.

Rules worth knowing

  • userRefId is set for actions taken by a signed-in person, and null for what clients, runs and WBSKT do on their own.
  • clientAddress and userAgent are kept beside the entry, never inside its data, and go with it at the end of retention.
  • A change's data holds changes: [{field, before, after}]. A shared variable's value set by hand is cut to 256 characters there.
  • Entries never carry internal ids, only the references the API uses.
  • Things that happen to a person (signing in, changing a password) are logged in every workspace they belong to.
