Workspaces and members

A workspace holds a project's boards, policies, workflows and integrations. Invite people to it and give each of them a role that says what they can do.

Invite someone

  1. Open Members from the workspace menu and choose Invite.
  2. Enter their email and pick a role. They get a link that works once.
  3. They sign in or sign up, and land in the workspace.
OwnerEvery workspace has one owner, who can't be removed. To leave a workspace you own, transfer ownership first.

Roles

Roles are sets of permissions. Every account starts with two: User, who can see everything in a workspace but change nothing, and Admin, who can do everything. Make your own for anything in between, like a role that can send commands but not edit workflows.

A role given everywhere applies in every workspace of the account; one given in a workspace applies only there.

When you're not allowed

A workspace you aren't in answers WORKSPACE_FORBIDDEN, whether or not it exists. Inside a workspace, a missing permission answers PERMISSION_UNAUTHORIZED, and the console hides or greys out what you can't do.
