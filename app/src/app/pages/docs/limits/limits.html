Sign-in and the API

Sign-in, sign-up and password calls are limited per address to 10 a minute, so a password can't be guessed by trying many. Refreshing a session is limited to 120 a minute. Past a limit the answer is 429 RATE_LIMITED with Retry-After.

What happens when…

I need more than a platform limit allows
Most platform limits are there to protect shared servers. Tell us what you are building; Business contracts can change several of them.
A board floods the gateway with readings
Past 10 a second the extra are dropped, and a board that keeps going is disconnected. The libraries pace for you, including the backlog they send after a reconnect.
The account hits its run limit
New runs are refused with PLAN_RUN_LIMIT_REACHED; a trigger's event shows as Refused and isn't queued.

Rules worth knowing

  • Plan limits on clients and runs are counted across all the account's workspaces under one lock per limit, so two workspaces one short of the limit can't both pass at the same instant.
  • A member is anyone in the account, suspended or not. A pending invitation counts once it is accepted.
  • The gateway never counts a pong answering a ping, or an acknowledgement of a command sent for confirmation, against a client's message rate.
