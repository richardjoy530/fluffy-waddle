How credits are counted

Starting a run costs 1 credit, test runs and runs started by Run workflow included. Then the run spends credits for each node it passes through, as listed in the node reference. Waiting is free, and so is the trigger.

NodeCreditsWhy
Starting a run1Every run writes its own record and history.
Triggers0Covered by the start charge.
If, loops, fork, join, variable, delay, waits, end, fail0.1Bookkeeping inside WBSKT.
Console notification0.5A message to open consoles.
Send to client, Run workflow1Crosses to a board or another run.
Telegram, Discord, ntfy, Call a URL5Leaves WBSKT over someone else's network.
Email10Each email has a real cost to send.
ExampleA rule that checks soil moisture, switches a pump and sends you a Telegram message costs 1 + 0.1 + 1 + 5 = 7.1 credits a run. Free's 2,000 credits cover about 280 runs a month.

What happens at a limit

On Free, Maker, Pro and Team, reaching a limit slows or pauses things. It never charges you. Business is the exception: it is never slowed, and use past the contract is invoiced, as the table says. You get a warning in the console and by email at 80% and 100%.

Paying and refunds

  • Payments are handled by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. Sales tax or VAT for your country is added at checkout.
  • Changed your mind? A monthly or yearly payment is refunded in full if you ask within 14 days of the charge.
  • After 14 days there are no refunds. Cancelling stops the next renewal, and you keep your plan until the end of the period you paid for.
  • A credit pack is refunded in full within 14 days of buying it, as long as none of its credits have been used. Credits that have been spent are not refunded.
  • Moving to a smaller plan doesn't delete anything at once. For 30 days, your readings and run history are kept for as long as your old plan kept them.
  • See your plan, usage and invoices under Plan and usage in the account menu.
