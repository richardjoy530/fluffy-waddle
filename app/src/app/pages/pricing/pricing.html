What is a workflow-second?

One second of a run actively doing something — checking a condition, sending a command, handling an event. Time spent waiting on a delay or an event is paused, and paused time is not billed.

What counts as a connected client?

Anything holding a live connection during the billing hour: a device, a backend service, a browser session, or a marketplace integration you have connected.

Is there really no per-task fee?

No. A workflow that fires 100,000 times and does about 20 milliseconds of work each time adds up to roughly 2,000 workflow-seconds — under two dollars. Priced per task, the same work would cost orders of magnitude more.

Can I cap what I spend?

Yes. Set a hard budget per workspace. When you reach it, new runs queue rather than bill, and your existing connections stay up.
