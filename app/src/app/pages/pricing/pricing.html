What is a workflow-second?

One second of a run actively executing — evaluating logic, sending commands, processing events. Time spent waiting on a delay or an event is paused and free.

What counts as a connection?

Any client holding a live socket during the billing hour: a device, a backend, a browser session, or a mounted marketplace integration.

Really no per-task fee?

Really. A workflow that fires 100,000 times doing 20ms of work each costs ~2,000 wf-sec — about $1.80. On per-task platforms that's a mortgage payment.

Can I cap my spend?

Yes. Hard budget limits per workspace — hit the cap and new runs queue instead of billing. Live connections stay up.
